## 1. Verdict

All three modules have unusually honest foundations, and each has one moment that would impress an engineer. In
Assembly, mates solve in about 0.1 s with SOLIDWORKS-style (f)/(−)/(+) states, and a conflicting mate is kept but not
applied. Interference volumes are exact, and the STEP export is re-import verified. In Image to CAD, a raster drawing
was read correctly (13 of 13 values with sensible meanings, 32 s), and the model came back with **exactly** the original
volume (58,885.7 mm³). Mesh Studio's live 8–10 s previews are easy to use. But the signature workflows break at the
seams a paying designer hits first:
- **Fastener auto-fit**, the Assembly headline, put an M5 nut and washer *inside* the mounting plate on the demo parts
  (2 interferences, 144 mm³). The hole recogniser also calls the L-bracket's through holes "blind".
- **Refreshing a part after a dimension change breaks every mate on that geometry.** That defeats the point of a
  parametric assembly.
- **One drawing-to-model run consumed 338 tokens**, against an on-screen estimate of "≈ 40", and drove a trial
  workspace to −38 tokens.
- The **first render takes 2.5 minutes with no explanation**. The "Dark" final render clips to pure white.

Today the modules demo well on a scripted path. A designer using their own parts would hit a wrong result or a dead
end within 10 minutes. Fix the P0s and the refresh/mate-repair loop before adding scope.

## 4. Keep / don't change

- **Assembly solver feedback**: mates solve in about 0.1 s. The (f)/(−)/(+)/⟳ prefixes, the "Over-defined · 1 conflicting"
  chip and the rule "conflicting mate kept but not applied, with residual" are exactly right (`a10_mated_conflict.png`).
  Hovering a mate highlights its references.
- **Fastener stack diagram and length derivation** (grip + washers + nut + 2P → ISO 888 length chips, ISO sources,
  VERIFY badge), and the explanation copy (`a18`/`a19`). Fix the probe, keep the presentation.
- **Interference check**: exact volumes, red clash bodies, click to focus, clearance table with Contact/Clear/
  Interference chips, 3.6 s for 11 pairs (`a26_check_result.png`). **BOM** with standards and masses, plus a clean CSV.
  **STEP export** with a re-import check in 1.3 s. The **explode** slider is sensible (`a23_explode.png`).
- **Mesh Studio**: material-suggested finish ("Material Carbon steel suggests Machined steel"), live 8–10 s previews
  that never block the controls, the honest "Visualisation render of a tessellated mesh" footer and per-render disclosure,
  the renderer/device line, and a final still in 45 s on an RTX 3070.
- **Image to CAD**: the "never guess scale" Scale card with evidence lines, numbered pins linked to rows, confidence
  badges, and "Show n answered". The vision read was excellent: Ø25/Ø20/40/20/30/150/(20)/40/9.5/6/(9.5)/(21.5)/3.5, each
  with a correct meaning, units and scale with evidence, in 32 s. The build round trip was exact (bbox 25×25×150,
  volume 58,885.7 mm³ = the original), with clean parameter names (COLLAR_DIA, KEYWAY_DEPTH…) and an honest
  assumptions list.

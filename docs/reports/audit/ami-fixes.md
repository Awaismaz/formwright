## Recommendations carried forward
- **AMI-I3 Change impact preview**: the pieces now exist (where-used, role-based remap, stack re-check). A dry-run
  refresh against a candidate build would give "2 mates follow, the M5 stack becomes M6×30" before the edit is
  accepted.
- **AMI-I5 Living datasheet pack**: the revision stamp and shared look are in place; a saved shot list re-rendered on
  a new revision is the next step.
- Whole-pattern Fasten proposes a stack in a hole that already holds one (probe ignores fasteners). Skip fitted holes.
- Warm-ups: Blender kernels and the assembly worker at server start (`app.py` hook), and a notification when a rebuild
  makes a pinned assembly instance stale (Studio rebuild path).
- Gizmo: restrict the drag axes to the solver's free directions instead of snapping back on drop.

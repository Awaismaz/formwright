# Formwright — Brand

## Name
**Formwright** = *form* (shape, design) + *wright* (a master maker, as in shipwright or millwright). It suggests craftsmanship and engineering competence, not a gadget. It's short and pronounceable, and it isn't tied to a single feature, so it still fits as modules are added.

**Status:** chosen by Claude under the founder's delegation (2026-11-16). **Trademark, company-name and domain availability are unverified** and are parked for the founder and counsel (PD-01). Domains used in the app (`formwright.dev`) are placeholders.

**Tagline:** "From idea to verified, manufacturable CAD."
**Category line:** "The AI design engineer for mechanical teams."

## Logo
- **Mark:** an isometric cube (the universal "part" glyph) in three blues, with a white F cut through the faces and a copper machined notch on the right face. The notch is the brand's "crafted" detail.
- **Wordmark:** "Form" in text colour plus "wright" in copper, set in Inter Semibold with tight tracking.
- **Files:** `web/public/favicon.svg`; React component `LogoMark` / `Logo` in `web/src/components/ui.tsx`.
- **Clear space:** at least half the mark's height. **Minimum size:** 16 px (favicon).

## Colour
The workspace palette is deliberately familiar to SOLIDWORKS users: graphite chrome, light-grey panels, blue selection, and a gradient graphics area. The brand marks and accents are our own, so there is no confusion with Dassault trade dress (no DS red, no SW logo forms).

| Token | Light | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Forge blue `--primary` | #1F5FBF | Primary actions, selection, links |
| Copper `--accent` | #C8742E | Brand accent, upgrade CTAs, God mode, Preview badges |
| Graphite `--chrome` | #23272E | Sidebar, title bars, dark sections |
| Panel / canvas | #FFFFFF / #ECEEF1 | Cards / app background |
| Viewport gradient | #CDD8E6 → #FCFDFF | 3D graphics area |
| Success / warning / danger | #1E874C / #C4800C / #C43228 | Check results and states |

A full dark theme is defined (Settings → Appearance).

## Typography
Inter Variable (OFL, self-hosted, no external font requests). Numbers use tabular figures in data. Code and parameters use the system monospace.

## Voice
Precise, calm, engineer-to-engineer. We say exactly what was checked and what wasn't. We never call outputs validated, certified or ready for manufacture. Preview features are always labelled. We use short sentences and units everywhere.
